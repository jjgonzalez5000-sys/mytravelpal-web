Join the closed beta

We're onboarding a small group of European Android drivers into the closed beta. If you're planning a road trip in the next few weeks and want to test the app before it hits Play Store publicly, we'd love your help.

What we need from you

  • An Android phone running Android 8 or newer
  • Google account for the beta invite (Play Store closed testing)
  • A real trip you're planning to take — the best feedback comes from actual use
  • Willingness to email us bugs and rough edges as you find them

What you get

  • Full Premium features unlocked during the beta
  • Direct line to the team — we implement good feedback quickly
  • Founding-user perks when we launch publicly

Sign up

Email hello@mytravelpal.io with:

  • Your Google account email (for the Play Store invite)
  • Country you live in
  • What kind of road trip you're planning (roughly)
  • Vehicle type (car / camper / motorbike / etc.)

We'll add you to the tester list within 24 hours and send you the invite link.
