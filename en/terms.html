1. What MyTravelPal does

MyTravelPal helps you plan road trips: route calculation, optimal departure time, stops to eat and sleep, toll and fuel estimates, weather along the route, hotel and restaurant suggestions, speed camera alerts (using the public OpenStreetMap database) and an AI driving assistant.

Estimates are approximations. Distances, driving times, tolls, fuel prices, hotel rates and speed camera alerts come from public sources and heuristic models. They may be inaccurate. Always cross-check critical information with official sources before committing to anything.

Speed camera alerts are informative, not active. The App queries a static database of speed cameras from OpenStreetMap and warns you of their approximate presence. It does not detect radar emissions (which would be illegal in several countries). Coverage and accuracy of the data depend on the OSM community and may be out of date. Do not rely solely on these alerts to decide your speed.

3. Subscriptions and billing

MyTravelPal offers three tiers:

  • Free: day-trip planning, one saved route, basic fuel and toll features.
  • Essential: multi-day trips with hotels and restaurants, unlimited saved trips, fuel arbitrage, electric vehicles.
  • Premium: everything in Essential plus AI Companion, speed cameras, group session, real-time alerts and Android Auto.

Subscriptions are managed through Google Play Billing. We do not process or store payment data. When you subscribe, Google sends us a purchase token to validate your access.

Prices: shown at the time of purchase inside the App. We may change them with 30 days’ notice, notifying you in the App.

Cancellation: you can cancel at any time from Google Play subscription management. Premium access remains active until the end of the paid period.

Refunds: follow Google Play’s standard policy (usually a 48-hour window from purchase).

Free trial: if a trial period is offered, it will be specified at the point of purchase. At the end of the trial you are billed automatically unless you cancel beforehand.

4. AI-generated content

The “Trip inspiration” features and the “Companion” assistant use language models (Anthropic Claude) to generate itinerary suggestions, answer questions and provide context while driving. Please note that:

  • Answers are indicative and may contain errors or out-of-date information.
  • They do not replace your own planning or the driver’s judgement.
  • Do not use the assistant to read long messages while driving; the interaction is designed for short, spoken answers.
  • Conversation history is not stored between sessions.

5. Companion mode and group sessions

Companion mode uses your device’s GPS in real time to give you alerts and contextual suggestions. If you create a group session, your position is shared with participants who have the access code. You are responsible for:

  • Obtaining your passengers’ consent before sharing the vehicle’s location with them.
  • Not using Companion mode in a way that distracts from driving.
  • Checking that your use of the assistant mode complies with local rules on using electronic devices while driving.

Group sessions expire automatically after 12 hours.
