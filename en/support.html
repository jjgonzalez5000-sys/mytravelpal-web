Support

Got a question, a bug report or a feature request? We are a small team and we read every message.

Email

  • General: hello@mytravelpal.io
  • Support and bug reports: support@mytravelpal.io
  • Privacy and data requests: privacy@mytravelpal.io
  • Security disclosure: security@mytravelpal.io (see security.txt)
  • Press: press@mytravelpal.io

Tester program

If you want early access to the app, sign up here. The iOS version is planned for late 2026.

Response time

We aim to reply within 3 business days. For urgent matters during a trip, please prefer emergency services (112 in the EU).

GDPR requests

You can export all your data and delete your account directly from the app: Settings → Download my data / Delete my account. For requests we cannot fulfil from within the app, email privacy@mytravelpal.io — we respond within 30 days.
