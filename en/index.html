EUROPEAN ROAD TRIPS · ANDROID

Optitrips plans
smart road trips across Europe.

We turn a plain «point A to point B» into a real itinerary: daily stages that respect your driving hours, meal and sleep stops, hotels near every overnight, the cheapest fuel stations on the route, real toll costs, weather along the way and the best departure time based on historical traffic.

Get the app See what it does

Your trip split into days

Tell it how long you want to drive each day and the app splits the route into stages, with breaks, meals and a place to sleep every night.

Clear tolls, and the ones you can skip

The toll cost of every day and, section by section, which ones you can skip and how much time that adds.

Refuel where it is cheapest

Based on your tank and consumption, it tells you where to refuel on the way and how much you save compared with stopping anywhere.

Best time to leave

Compare departure times to see when you meet less traffic and arrive sooner.

Where to sleep each night

Hotels around the real point where each stage ends, not only at the destination, booked in one tap.

Weather and warnings on your route

Hour-by-hour forecast where you will pass, official weather warnings, wildfires and other risks before you leave and while you drive.

A companion on the road

Spoken alerts, nearby stops and a trip summary, also on the car screen with Android Auto.

Motorhomes and electric cars

Vehicle dimensions, AdBlue and fast chargers on the route with their power and connectors.
