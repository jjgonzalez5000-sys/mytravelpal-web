Route with real tolls

Every plan shows the exact toll cost per motorway and segment, not a rough estimate. It is easy to compare the fast route against the free national road.

Best departure time

We analyse historical traffic around your target window and suggest the sweet spot: leaving 30 minutes earlier can save 90 minutes on the road.

Hotels along the route

For every overnight stop the planner picks, you get a list of nearby hotels — not just at the final destination — with direct links to Booking.

Fuel arbitrage

Petrol and diesel prices in real time from the official government feeds in ES/FR/IT. We alert you when the next station saves 8-15 % compared with the closest one.

Adverse events along the route

Wildfires (EFFIS), floods and weather warnings cross-referenced with the day’s itinerary. If anything crosses your path, the alert reaches you before you leave.

Three screens from the main flow: plan, result and companion mode on the road.
