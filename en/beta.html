Tester programme

Optitrips is in closed testing on Google Play. If you have an Android phone and would like to use the app on your trips and tell us what you would improve, we would love to have you on board.

How to join

  1. Ask us for access: write to hello@optitrips.com from the Google account you use on the Play Store (or tell us which one). We add you to the tester list within 24 hours.
  2. Opt in on Play once we confirm you are in: use the button below and tap “Become a tester”.
  3. Install Optitrips from the Play link on that same page. If Play does not show it yet, wait a few minutes and try again.

Join on Google Play

What you need

  • An Android 8 or newer phone with Google Play.
  • A Google account (the one you use on Play).

What you get

  • Early access to the app and to every new feature before anyone else.
  • A direct line to the people who build it: good feedback lands fast.

Questions or bugs?

Write to hello@optitrips.com or use “Send a suggestion” in the app settings.
