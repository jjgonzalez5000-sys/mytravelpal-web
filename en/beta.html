Tester program

We onboard a small group of European Android drivers at a time. If you have a road trip planned in the next few weeks and want to use the app on the road and share your feedback, we would love to have you on board.

What we need from you

  • An Android phone running Android 8 or newer.
  • A Google account for the invite (Play Store closed track).
  • A real trip you are about to take — the best feedback comes from actual use.
  • Willingness to email us bugs and rough edges as you find them.

What you get

  • All Premium features unlocked while you test.
  • Direct line to the team — we ship good feedback quickly.
  • Founding-user perks when we open the app to the general public.

How to sign up

Email hello@mytravelpal.io with:

  • Your Google account email (for the Play Store invite).
  • Country you live in.
  • What kind of road trip you are planning (roughly).
  • Vehicle type (car / camper / motorbike / etc.).

We add you to the tester list within 24 hours and send you the invite link.
