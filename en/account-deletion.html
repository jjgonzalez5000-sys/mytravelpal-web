Delete your MyTravelPal account

Last updated: 2026-09-28

MyTravelPal is a road-trip planning Android app. You can request deletion of your account and every piece of data linked to it at any time. This page describes both self-service (in-app) and manual (email) paths.

Option 1 — In-app deletion (recommended)

This is the fastest route. It runs synchronously against our backend and removes every server-side row we hold for your account.

  1. Open the MyTravelPal app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. Scroll to Account → Delete my account.
  4. Read the confirmation dialog, then tap Delete permanently.
  5. The app calls DELETE /v1/me/account, wipes the local trip storage and signs you out.

You will land on the login screen. Any device that was signed in with the same account is signed out on its next open.

Option 2 — Email request

If you can't open the app (for example the phone is lost or you've already uninstalled), send an email to privacy@mytravelpal.io with:

  • Subject: Account deletion request
  • The email address you used to sign in (Google or Facebook)
  • Optional: the approximate date of your last login

We reply within 7 working days confirming the deletion. GDPR requires we complete the request within 30 days at most.

What gets deleted

  • Profile: your traveller preferences (departure hour, driving cap, meal windows, dietary flags, hotel & restaurant preferences).
  • Saved trips: every trip you saved from the result screen, including the tweaks you re-saved (picked hotels, fuel stops, day overrides).
  • Garage vehicles: every car you added, with tank size and fuel-type defaults.
  • Emergency contacts: the phones you added for the crash-detection SOS flow (if any).
  • Authentication rows: the account record itself, the JWT session tokens on our servers, and the OAuth link to Google or Facebook.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Anonymous crash logs collected by Sentry keep a hashed device fingerprint for 90 days for stability triage — they carry no personal data.
  • Aggregated, non-identifiable metrics (e.g. "N plans computed on 2026-09-25", "median trip distance in FR") are kept indefinitely for product analytics. These cannot be re-associated with an individual account.
  • Legal / financial obligations: if you made a payment (currently none — we're free during beta), invoicing records are kept for the period required by Spanish tax law (4-6 years) with only your billing name and amount.

Everything else is deleted — there is no cold-storage backup of your account after the deletion completes.

Just delete some data, keep the account?

You don't have to delete everything to trim the data we hold on you. From the app you can:

  • Delete a single saved trip: Trips tab → long-press a trip → Delete.
  • Delete a garage vehicle: Profile → My garage → swipe left on a vehicle.
  • Clear recent searches: Profile → Privacy → Clear recent searches.

Full data-handling detail is on the Privacy Policy. Questions? privacy@mytravelpal.io.
