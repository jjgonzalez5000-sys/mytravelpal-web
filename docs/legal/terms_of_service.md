## 3. Fuel prices and other data

Fuel prices come from official government feeds (Spain Ministerio,
France Roulez-Éco, Italy MITE) refreshed daily. Prices displayed in
the app can lag actual pump prices by up to 24 hours. Verify at the
pump before refuelling if the price matters to you.

Adverse events (fires, roadworks) come from NASA FIRMS and other
public sources. False positives and missing events are inherent to
these datasets. Use them as one input among many.
