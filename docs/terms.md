## 1. What MyTravelPal does

MyTravelPal helps you plan road trips: route computation, optimal
departure time, meal and sleep stops, toll and fuel cost estimates,
weather along the route, and hotel/restaurant suggestions.

**Estimates are approximations.** Distances, driving times, tolls,
fuel prices and hotel rates come from public data sources (OSM/OSRM,
government fuel feeds, Open-Meteo, Geoapify) and heuristic models.
They can be wrong. Always cross-check critical information (toll
booth prices, hotel availability, road closures) with authoritative
sources before you commit.
