Response time

We aim to reply within 3 business days. For urgent matters during a trip, please prefer emergency services (112 in the EU).

Data requests (GDPR)

You can export all your data and delete your account directly from the app: Settings → Download my data / Delete my account. For requests we can't fulfil from within the app, email privacy@mytravelpal.io — we respond within 30 days.
