## Full description (max 4000 chars — this is 2350)
```
Plan the drive, not just the destination.

MyTravelPal turns "point A to point B" into a real road-trip itinerary — daily stages that respect your driving hours, meal times and bedtime, with hotels near every overnight stop, cheapest fuel along the route, real toll costs, weather along the way and traffic-aware departure times.

WHY MYTRAVELPAL
Google Maps tells you the fastest route. MyTravelPal tells you the SMART route: when to leave to avoid traffic, where to sleep on multi-day trips, where fuel is cheapest, what the weather will be tomorrow, and which tolls you can skip.

KEY FEATURES

Multi-day itinerary
• Automatic day splits that respect your daily driving cap, bedtime, wake time and meal preferences.
• Every overnight stop suggests hotels within driving distance, filtered by stars, price, parking and pet-friendly.
• Meal recommendations along the route, with cuisine filters.

Real fuel prices
• Live government fuel-price feeds in Spain, France and Italy.
• Pan-European coverage for Germany, Portugal, Benelux, Austria, Switzerland, Poland and beyond via HERE data.
• The cheapest station along your route, not a random one.

Real tolls
• Per-country toll estimates in EUR based on HERE routing.
• Toll-free alternative route on demand, with time-vs-cost comparison.
• Partial toll skips: which specific booths you can bypass without adding hours.

Adverse-event alerts
• Wildfires, floods, heavy rain, thunderstorms, snow, road closures, earthquakes.
• Critical alerts arrive as high-priority notifications while you drive.

Best time to leave
• Traffic-window analysis: sometimes leaving 30 min earlier saves 90 min on the road.
• Historical + forecast traffic combined per country.

AI Trip Inspiration
• Describe your dream trip in one line ("romantic weekend in Loire castles") and the AI drafts a full multi-day itinerary you can turn into a real plan in one tap.

Companion mode
• Speed + drive-time tracker with pause reminders.
• Push-to-talk voice commands: "cheapest fuel", "next hotel", "skip this toll".
• Automatic crash detection with SOS to your emergency contacts.

Waypoints with dwell time
• Add intermediate stops with "stay 2 nights here" and the planner splits your trip accordingly.

Multi-language
Spanish, English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Catalan.

FREEMIUM
Free forever for basic planning, hotels and fuel. Premium unlocks unlimited AI generations, live crash detection with SOS, "Leave Now" quick plan, real hotel prices and more (€3.50/month).

PRIVACY-FIRST
No ad tracking. No location sold. Full GDPR data export and account deletion in-app.

Ready for your next road trip? mytravelpal.io
