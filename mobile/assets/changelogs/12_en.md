## What's new in 0.2.9

- **This very panel** — you'll now see release notes every time we ship an update.
- **Cross-border hotel savings** — when one country is pricier than its neighbour we flag it inside the affected day card with the estimated saving.
- **Forced food break on long trips** — even when the trip doesn't cross your usual meal window, we now propose a midway restaurant stop for trips ≥4 h (≥3 h with kids or pets).
- **EV cars and chargers** — 40 new EV models plus charging stations along the route.
- **Full POI card in Maps** when you tap a hotel or restaurant.
- **Cleaner day view** — removed the redundant "You'll sleep at…" card.
- **Fewer crashes** — fonts bundled in the APK, no more Google Fonts network dependency.
