### What's new in this version

**One Recalculate button**
Added stops, edited day times and skipped tolls are now applied together with a single "Recalculate route" button that shows what is pending.

**Skip tolls for real**
Skipping tolls now recomputes the route, the stops and the day plan on the road you will actually drive, with a way back to the route with tolls.

**Edit when you stop driving**
Changing the end time of a day always applies, and you are told how many km move to the next day or days (or come forward from them).

**AI suggestions, tidied up**
Suggestions, your places and "Add your own stop" live in one collapsible section. Stops show an "AI" tag and an approximate time you can change.

**Your places**
Places you save are no longer lost when your profile syncs, and AI suggestions are no longer saved into them automatically.
