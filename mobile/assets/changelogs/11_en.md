## What's new in 0.2.8

- **Cross-border hotel savings** — when one country is pricier than its neighbour, we flag it inside the day card with an estimated saving. Same idea as the fuel-arbitrage banner but for overnights.
- **Forced food break on long trips** — even when the trip doesn't cross your usual meal window, we now propose a midway restaurant stop for trips ≥4 h (≥3 h with kids or pets).
- **EV cars and chargers** — 40 new EV models in the catalogue (Tesla, VW ID, Kia EV6, Hyundai Ioniq, BMW iX/i4, MG4, BYD, Polestar…) plus charging stations along the route.
- **Full POI card in Maps** — tapping a hotel or restaurant now opens the correct POI card directly in Google Maps (before you had to hunt for the POI after landing on the coordinate).
- **Cleaner day view** — removed the redundant "You'll sleep at…" card that showed twice under the day summary.
- **Fewer crashes** — fonts are now bundled in the APK, no more Google Fonts network dependency.
