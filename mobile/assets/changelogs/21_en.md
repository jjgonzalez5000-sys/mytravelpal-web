## What's new in 0.2.18

- **Weather alerts with context** — every alert now shows the source (Open-Meteo, EFFIS, USGS…), when it was detected, valid until when, and the estimated duration. No more cryptic warnings.
- **Consistent language for rest stops** — the reason each rest stop was picked ("kid-friendly area matching your profile", etc.) is now translated to your app language, not stuck in English.
- **Unified day-card loading indicator** — the spinner + italic caption is the same for restaurants, fuel / EV chargers, hotels, and rest areas. No more duplicate messages when breakfast + lunch + dinner are all loading.
- **Confirm before exiting** — pressing back on the main screen now shows "Leave MyTravelPal?" with an extra warning if Companion mode is running.
- **Tighter UI** — "Add stop" and "Reverse" now sit on the same row, saving a bit of scroll on the planner.
