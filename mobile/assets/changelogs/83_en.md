### What's new in this version

**Visual time picker**
Adjust your driving window with a 24-hour bar showing real sunrise and sunset for your route location.

**Weather along route**
Hourly forecast per segment: icon + temperature at key points along your route (Open-Meteo).

**Interactive day bars**
Tap any day bar to edit its schedule directly. Bars show a shimmer while replanning to prevent accidental taps.

**Faster day adjustments**
Adjusting day times now uses the fast endpoint — the new structure appears in ~3 s while hotels/fuel update in parallel.

**Preference-aware quality score**
Trip quality now adapts to your settings: electric vehicle, overnight policy, driving pace.

**Sticky search progress banner**
The "searching hotels / fuel / alerts" banner stays fixed at the top so it doesn't scroll away.
