### What's new in this version

**More reliable tolls**
Tolls in countries with another currency (Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Switzerland) are now converted to euros correctly. When they cannot be obtained we say so instead of showing a made-up figure.

**Legend for the stage breakdown**
The colours of the bars are now explained.

**Vehicle always from your garage**
The trip profile uses the garage vehicle (type, fuel and consumption) instead of separate settings.

**More detail on the stages**
Each day now lists meals, dinners and service stops with their time and name, and the end of each day names the town and region instead of just the street.
