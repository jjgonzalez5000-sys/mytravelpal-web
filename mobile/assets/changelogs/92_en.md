### What's new in this version

**AI stop suggestions (Premium)**
Real places near your route that fit your free time, picked and explained by AI. Tick it on the plan form and the best picks appear as soon as your route is ready.

**My places**
Save the spots you love; whenever a route passes near one, it is offered as a stop.

**Trip CO₂**
Estimated emissions of the drive, total and per passenger.

**Vignettes, country by country**
Price per country, digital or sticker, and a link to the official seller.

**Fuel prices you can trust**
Stations no longer show LPG or E85 prices as petrol or diesel.

**Safer replans**
If changing the route would drop a hotel, restaurant or fuel pick, we ask you first.
