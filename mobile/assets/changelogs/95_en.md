### What's new in this version

**Android Auto: correct speed and distance**
The speed row on the car screen no longer shows the remaining distance as if it were your speed.

**Free navigation on the car screen**
When you leave the planned route, the car screen shows only your speed and position instead of frozen route figures.

**Rain and storm alerts in the car**
Weather warnings now pop up on the car screen and are listed in the app, and the check includes the spot where you are right now, not only the road ahead.
