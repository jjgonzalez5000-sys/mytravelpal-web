## What's new in 0.2.17

- **Uniform loading indicators** — every section on a day card (restaurants, rest areas, hotels) now shows the same style while loading: a small spinner + italic status text. No more static gray blocks.
- **Real shimmer on skeletons** — loading placeholders now feature a smooth light-band sweep instead of the previous subtle colour pulse. You can tell the app is actually working.
- **Feedback system fully operational** — suggestions sent from the app now land in hello@mytravelpal.io by email on top of being stored in the database.
