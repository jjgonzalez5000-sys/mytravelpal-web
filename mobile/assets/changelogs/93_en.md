### What's new in this version

**Add any vehicle**
A step-by-step wizard for cars, motorbikes, vans, campers and trucks, with size and weight where it matters. The vehicle you pick now sets the type and engine of your trip.

**AI suggestions, with details**
Tap a suggestion to see it on the map with your route, its address, phone, website and opening hours.

**AdBlue prices**
Stations that sell AdBlue show its price per litre.

**Correct petrol and diesel prices abroad**
Fixed a mix-up that quoted diesel prices to petrol drivers in Germany, Austria and Switzerland, and the other way round.

**Honest tolls**
When the exact toll cannot be confirmed, you now see a rough estimate marked with ≈, and it is much closer to reality on multi-country trips.

**Light theme**
The trip summary is readable again in the light theme.
